His web page does not have a download link, just a note to email him for a copy. I _did_ receive a copy directly from Harry Dodgson after emailing him. (Way to go Harry!) I've asked him if it would be OK to post it at UNNA. It is a nice basic utility to show the lat lon, speed, time (corrected for your chosen time zone), and heading. It can set the Newton's time to the GPS time. It only works in landscape mode. (it was written on an e-Mate after all...)(...)Thanks,Walter Johnson
